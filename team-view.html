FINAL AUCTION — TERMS & CONDITIONS

⭐ 1. Player Selection

Players are selected based on their performance in the last two IPL seasons.

📈 2. Bid Increment Rules

  1. Till ₹1 lakh: Every next bid increases by ₹10,000.
  2. From ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh: Every next bid increases by ₹50,000.
  3. Above ₹5 lakh: Every next bid increases by ₹1,00,000.

💰 3. Bidding & Budget

  • All bids must remain within the team's available budget.
  • The highest valid bid wins the player.
  • Once a player is declared SOLD, the purchase is final.
  • The bid amount will be deducted from the team's Remaining Budget.

🏆 4. Winner Criteria

  • Only Eligible Teams will be considered for the final result.
  • Remaining Budget will be considered in the final winner determination.
  • In case of a tie, the team with the higher Remaining Budget will rank higher.
  • If the tie continues, Total Spent will be considered.

🏏 5. Team Eligibility

  • Each team must purchase 4 players: 1 Batsman • 1 Bowler • 1 Wicketkeeper • 1 All-Rounder.
  • All four categories must be completed to become Eligible.
  • Only one player from each category may be purchased by a team.

🤝 6. Fair Play

  • Use only your assigned Team Access/PIN.
  • Fake bidding, unauthorized access, or system manipulation is strictly prohibited.
  • All participants must follow the instructions of the Auctioneer and Organizing Committee.

🔒 7. Final Authority

The decision of the Official Auction Committee regarding bidding, player allocation, eligibility, ranking, technical issues, or disputes shall be final.
